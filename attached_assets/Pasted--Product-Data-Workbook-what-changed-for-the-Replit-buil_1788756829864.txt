## 2. The content model changed

The product page now has four distinct copy layers instead of one. Each says something different — none repeats another:

1. **`tagline`** — one line under the H1. Fragment style, no full stop, ≤60 chars.
2. **`blurb`** — short intro paragraph.
3. **`key_attributes`** — 5–6 bullets.
4. **`description`** — long-form body, 3–5 paragraphs, 148–244 words. **Plain paragraphs, no headings** (per brief §4.2 "paragraphs preserved"). Preserve line breaks.

**All 150 descriptions were rewritten.** They no longer contain:

- rainfall, pH, sowing rate, sowing depth, soil range, end use or livestock — these render in the quick-facts bar above
- grazing management, disease/pest, or stand-life content — these moved to their own columns (below) and render as the three accordions beneath the description
- breeder or supplier company names (see §4)

`description_source` values are now all `Rewritten from …` and remain **admin-only, not published**.

## 4. Two fields are now admin-only — do not render

`bred_by_origin` (K) and `supplier_name` (AM) must **never** appear on a public page. They name breeders and suppliers of on-sold lines, which would point customers at IH Seeds' own suppliers.

The build brief and the backend fields doc have both been updated to say this (brief §1.1, §4.2, and the import mapping table).

Related editorial rule now applied throughout the copy: a description may say **why** a variety was bred, never **who** bred it — unless the breeder is a research body (a state department of agriculture, SARDI, QDPI, CLIMA), which is fine to name.

---

## 5. Import notes

- `tagline` replaces `summary` in the importer's field mapping. The SEO fallback in §4.4 (meta description falls back to summary) should now read `blurb`, which is better suited to it than the ≤60-char tagline.
- `key_attributes` splits on `|` with no spaces around the delimiter.
- `description` contains `\n` line breaks that must survive import — paragraphs are meaningful.
- `blurb`, `key_attributes` and `distribution_note` are new fields to add to the products table and to the **Content & publishing** tab of the admin form (§2.1 item 5).
