# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public IH Seeds catalogue, availability, regional advice, guide-download, and enquiry landing experience.

## Out of scope
- Authenticated administration and internal tools.
- API-only endpoints except where they affect public crawlability or page rendering.

## Target audience
- Farmers and agricultural customers seeking pasture seed products, availability, and regional agronomic advice.

## Primary keywords
- Pasture seeds
- Seed availability
- Regional pasture advice
- Irwin Hunter seeds
